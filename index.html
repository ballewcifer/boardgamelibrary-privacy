Privacy Policy — Board Game Library

Last updated: 2026-07-28

Board Game Library ("the app") is a personal board-game lending-library tracker for Android and iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Summary

  • The app has no user accounts and no developer-run servers.
  • All of your library data — games, members, checkouts, and play history — is stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database.
  • The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.
  • The only network requests the app makes are to BoardGameGeek.com ("BGG"), and only when you choose to sync your collection or search for a game.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter into the app — your game collection, the members you add, checkout/return history, and play logs — is stored only in a local database on your device. It is never transmitted to the developer.

Data sent to BoardGameGeek

If you use the "Sync with BGG" feature, the app sends your BGG username (and, if you provide it, your BGG password or API token) directly to BoardGameGeek's own servers, to retrieve your collection. This exchange is between your device and BGG — the developer of this app never sees or stores your BGG credentials or data. See BoardGameGeek's own privacy policy for how they handle that information. Your BGG password, if entered, is stored only in your device's secure, encrypted credential storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) — never in the app's regular database or in any backup file.

Photos

If you choose to set a custom cover photo for a game, the app opens your device's photo picker. The photo you pick is copied into the app's local storage for display — it is never uploaded anywhere.

Backup & export

The app has an in-app "Export Backup" feature that creates a JSON file of your library data on your device, which you can then save or share anywhere you choose using your device's normal share sheet — this is entirely under your control. On Android, the operating system's standard Auto Backup feature may also include the app's local data in your device's own Google account backup; this is a standard Android OS feature, governed by your own Google account and Google's policies, not something this app configures or accesses independently.

Children's privacy

Board Game Library is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data deletion

You can delete all app data at any time from the app's Settings menu ("Clear All Data"), or by uninstalling the app, which removes its local database entirely.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Material changes will be reflected in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be raised via GitHub Issues.
